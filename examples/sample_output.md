# FDA 510(k) Summary Prototype: BP-Next Gen

## 1. Device Description
The BP-Next Gen is a non-invasive, automated digital sphygmomanometer intended for the measurement of systolic and diastolic blood pressure in the brachial artery. The device utilizes an oscillometric method with an integrated digital signal processing (DSP) module for noise filtration during cuff inflation.

## 2. Predicate Device
The BP-Next Gen is substantially equivalent to the Omron HEM-7120 (K123456). The predicate device is a cleared automated digital blood pressure monitor.

## 3. Comparison of Technological Characteristics
| Feature | BP-Next Gen | Omron HEM-7120 | Conclusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Measurement Method | Oscillometric | Oscillometric | Equivalent |
| Target Population | Adult Home User | Adult Home User | Equivalent |
| Data Processing | AI-based Noise Filtering | Standard Analog Filtering | Different, but no new safety concerns |
| Display | Digital LCD | Digital LCD | Equivalent |

## 4. Performance Testing
The BP-Next Gen was evaluated according to the ISO 81060-2:2018 standard for the validation of non-invasive sphygmomanometers. 

**Test Summary:**
- **Sample Size**: N=85 subjects.
- **Mean Difference**: 1.2 mmHg.
- **Standard Deviation**: 2.1 mmHg.
- **Result**: The device demonstrated a mean error of +/- 3mmHg, which is within the acceptable limits of the ISO 81060-2 standard.

## 5. Conclusion
Based on the provided data, the BP-Next Gen is substantially equivalent to the predicate device in terms of intended use and technological characteristics. The inclusion of AI-based noise filtering does not introduce new questions of safety or effectiveness.
